---

Moving a committee off paper is not about the technology. It is about the gains
that follow — better decisions, less wasted time, and a board that can see what is
going on. Here is what changes when a not-for-profit board goes digital.

## Better insights

Going digital not only saves paper but helps to collect important data on the
everyday operations of the not-for-profit. Equipped with this data and good
analytical tools, committee members are able to make better decisions on their
current activities and events.

## Work anywhere

With the move from physical to digital, documents can be accessed anywhere,
anytime. This saves countless hours and helps all committee members be prepared
for the next meeting.

## Faster communication

When it comes to board efficiency, communication plays a large role in helping the
committee run their meetings. With digital communication tools, sharing updates
and information becomes faster and more accessible.

## Encourage transparency

Transparency keeps the morale of committee members and stakeholders high because
it increases the integrity of the group. With digital tools, keeping the right
people informed becomes easier and faster.
