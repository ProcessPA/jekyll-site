I built Process PA to handle my own board paperwork.

I'm Matthew Rowan. I sat on boards and committees as a volunteer, and the paperwork always fell to whoever put their hand up — building the agenda, getting the papers out, writing up the minutes and chasing the actions before the next meeting.

There was plenty of advice on how a board should run, but nothing that did the repetitive work. So I built Process PA to guide the prep and turn my notes into signed minutes — for my own board first.

Today it does the same for {{ site.customer_count }} clubs, associations and not-for-profits across Australia — run by capable people who have better things to do than wrestle with Word templates.
