What are board papers?

Board papers — the board pack — are the documents directors receive ahead of a meeting: the agenda, financial reports, the previous minutes, and any supporting papers for decisions to be made. The quality and timeliness of the board pack largely determines the quality of the meeting. Directors who receive a complete pack in advance arrive ready to decide; directors who see papers for the first time in the room can only react.

The problem with manual board packs

Assembling a board pack by hand is one of the most time-consuming jobs a secretary does: collecting reports from different people, merging PDFs, getting the order right, and emailing a large bundle that some inboxes reject. When a document changes at the last minute, the whole pack has to be rebuilt and resent. It is slow, error-prone, and easy to get wrong.

What board papers software does

  • Builds the agenda progressively — add items across the week as they arise
  • Attaches documents to agenda items — reports and papers sit with the item they relate to
  • Bundles everything into one board pack — combined PDFs, correctly ordered
  • Distributes securely — by email or online, with no oversized attachments to wrangle
  • Stores the pack against the meeting — so the full history is always retrievable
