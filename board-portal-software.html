What is a board portal?

A board portal replaces emailed attachments, printed board packs and scattered files with a single secure system. It typically handles:

  • Board papers and agendas — prepared, bundled and distributed to directors before the meeting
  • Minutes — taken during the meeting and distributed straight afterwards
  • Motions and resolutions — recorded with movers, seconders and outcomes
  • Action items — assigned with owners and due dates, then tracked between meetings
  • Registers and documents — members, motions and actions kept current, with secure document storage

Who needs a board portal?

In Australia, board portals are no longer just for large listed companies. Any organisation with a board or management committee that meets regularly benefits from one, including:

  • Incorporated associations — trade, professional and community associations
  • Not-for-profits and charities registered with the ACNC
  • Sporting and community clubs, RSL and leagues clubs, golf and bowls clubs
  • School P&Cs and parent committees
  • Body corporate committees with BCCM Act record-keeping obligations
  • Small business and start-up boards

What to look for in a board portal

When choosing board portal software for an Australian organisation, the factors that matter most are:

  • Ease of use — volunteer office bearers change regularly, so the software must be simple to learn and hand over
  • Fair pricing — per-organisation pricing (not per-director) keeps costs predictable as your board grows
  • Local compliance — support for the record-keeping expected under state Associations Incorporation Acts and the BCCM Act
  • Security — encrypted, cloud-based document storage
  • Works everywhere — any browser, any device, with email-only access for members who prefer it
