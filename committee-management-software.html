What is committee management software?

Committee management software brings the whole governance cycle into one system. Instead of agendas in one place, minutes in another and action items in someone's notebook, the committee runs everything — before, during and after the meeting — from a single tool:

  • Before: prepare and distribute the agenda and board papers
  • During: take minutes, record motions and resolutions, assign actions
  • After: distribute minutes, track actions to completion, keep the records

Committee compliance in Australia

Australian incorporated associations have real record-keeping obligations under their state's Associations Incorporation Act — minutes of all meetings, a current register of members, and accurate financial records. Body corporate committees have similar obligations under the BCCM Act. Committee management software keeps these records complete, consistent and accessible, so the committee can demonstrate good governance rather than scrambling to reconstruct it when an audit, dispute or handover arrives.

Built for volunteer committees

Committee office bearers are usually volunteers, and they change regularly. That makes two things essential: the software has to be easy to use, and handovers have to be simple. Process PA is designed around both — new office bearers are guided through their role and inherit a complete governance history, so nothing is lost when the secretary or treasurer changes.
