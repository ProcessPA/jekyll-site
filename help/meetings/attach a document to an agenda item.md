## Overview

You can attach supporting documents — reports, financials, papers — directly to an agenda item. Attached documents travel with the agenda, including when it's emailed, so members have everything they need in one place.

## Step-by-Step Guide

1. Open the meeting and select the agenda item.
2. Choose to attach a document.
3. Pick a file from your committee's document library, or upload one. See [Upload a file](/help/documents/upload%20a%20file).
4. Save. The document is now linked to that agenda item.

## What happens when you send the agenda

When you email the agenda, attached documents are bundled and delivered as a ZIP pack alongside the agenda PDF and a calendar invite, so recipients receive the papers with the agenda. See [Agenda and minutes attachments](/help/meetings/email%20attachments).
