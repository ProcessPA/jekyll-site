## Overview

When you email an agenda or minutes from Process PA, the right attachments are bundled automatically. Members receive a complete, professional pack in their inbox — they don't even need to log in.

## What an agenda email carries

- **The agenda PDF** — matching the on-screen preview exactly.
- **A calendar invite (.ics)** — so members can add the meeting to their calendar in one click.
- **A ZIP pack of attached documents** — any documents you attached to agenda items are bundled together and delivered inside a single ZIP, with the files genuinely included.

## What a minutes email carries

- **The minutes PDF** — matching the on-screen preview exactly.

## Step-by-Step Guide

1. Attach any supporting documents to your agenda items. See [Attach a document to an agenda item](/help/meetings/attach%20a%20document%20to%20an%20agenda%20item).
2. Preview the agenda or minutes to check it reads correctly. See [Preview and download the PDF](/help/meetings/preview%20and%20download%20pdf).
3. Send the agenda or minutes. The PDF, calendar invite and document ZIP (for agendas) are attached automatically.

## Good to know

- Because everything a member needs arrives by email, they get the full benefit of Process PA without logging in.
- The attached PDF always reflects the latest preview, so recipients and your on-screen copy stay in sync.
