## Overview

Every meeting in Process PA moves through a predictable set of states. Understanding them makes it clear which actions are available at any moment — and why some are deliberately blocked. This keeps your records consistent and your minutes trustworthy.

## The states

- **Scheduled** — created but not yet under way.
- **Started** — running; minutes are being taken and the timer is counting.
- **Paused** — temporarily halted; the timer is stopped.
- **Closed** — the meeting has ended but isn't yet locked.
- **Finalized** — the minutes are locked as the formal record.

## The lifecycle

A typical meeting flows like this:

**Start → Pause → Resume → Reset → Close → Re-open → Finalize → Sign**

Alongside that flow, a scheduled meeting can also be **Re-scheduled** to a new date or **Cancelled**.

| Action | What it does | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| Start | Begins a scheduled meeting | [Start a meeting](/help/meetings/start%20a%20meeting) |
| Pause / Resume | Stops and restarts the timer | [Pause and resume](/help/meetings/pause%20and%20resume%20a%20meeting) |
| Reset | Returns a started meeting to scheduled | [Reset a meeting](/help/meetings/reset%20a%20meeting) |
| Close | Ends the meeting, with or without finalizing | [Close a meeting](/help/meetings/close%20a%20meeting) |
| Re-open | Reopens a closed meeting for changes | [Re-open a meeting](/help/meetings/re-open%20a%20meeting) |
| Finalize / Sign | Locks and signs the minutes | [Finalize and sign](/help/meetings/finalize%20a%20meeting) |
| Re-schedule | Moves a scheduled meeting | [Re-schedule](/help/meetings/re-schedule%20a%20meeting) |
| Cancel | Cancels a meeting | [Cancel a meeting](/help/meetings/cancel%20a%20meeting) |

## Which actions are valid when

Process PA only offers the actions that make sense for the current state, and blocks the rest:

- A **scheduled** meeting can be started, re-scheduled or cancelled — but not paused, closed or signed, because it hasn't begun.
- A **finalized** meeting can't be edited, re-opened or cancelled — its minutes are the locked record.
- **Finalizing requires the meeting opener to be configured** first. See [Configure the meeting opener](/help/meetings/configure%20the%20opener%20of%20a%20meeting).

These guardrails mean you can't accidentally take an action that would compromise the integrity of your minutes.
