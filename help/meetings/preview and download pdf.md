## Overview

Before sending anything, you can preview a meeting on screen and download a PDF. For a scheduled meeting this is the **agenda**; for a completed meeting it's the **minutes**. The generated PDF matches the on-screen preview exactly, so what you see is what your members receive.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### 1. Preview on screen
1. Open the meeting.
2. Choose to preview it. A scheduled meeting shows its agenda; a completed meeting shows its minutes.
3. When you're done, navigate back to the meeting.

### 2. Download the PDF
1. From the preview, choose to download the PDF.
2. The agenda PDF (or minutes PDF) is generated and downloaded, matching the preview you just saw.

## Good to know

- The same PDF is attached automatically when you email the agenda or minutes, so recipients always get a copy that matches the preview.
- Agenda emails also include a calendar invite and a ZIP pack of any attached documents. See [Agenda and minutes attachments](/help/meetings/email%20attachments).
