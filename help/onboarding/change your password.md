## Overview

If you're already logged in and want to change your password — for example on a regular schedule, or because you suspect it's no longer private — you can do it yourself from **User > Manage**. You don't need a reset email; you simply confirm your current password and choose a new one. If you can't log in because you've forgotten your password, use [Reset a forgotten password](/help/onboarding/reset%20your%20password) instead.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### 1. Open User > Manage
1. Log in to Process PA at [https://app.processpa.com/](https://app.processpa.com/).
2. Select your name in the top navigation, then choose **User**.
3. Select **Manage** to open your account details.

### 2. Change your password
1. Enter your **current password** to confirm it's you.
2. Enter your **new password**.
3. Confirm the new password by typing it a second time.
4. Save your changes.

Your new password takes effect immediately. The next time you log in, use your new password.

## Tips

- Choose a strong, unique password that you don't use on other sites.
- For an extra layer of security, ask about [two-factor authentication](/help/onboarding/two-factor%20authentication).
- If you're ever locked out, you can always [reset a forgotten password](/help/onboarding/reset%20your%20password) by email.
