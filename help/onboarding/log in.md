## Overview

This guide covers signing in to Process PA, staying signed in with "remember me", and what to expect when your details don't match an account. Process PA runs entirely in your browser, so you can log in from any device at any time.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### 1. Open the login page
1. Go to [https://app.processpa.com/](https://app.processpa.com/). If you are not already signed in, you will be taken to the login page.

### 2. Enter your details
1. Type the email address you registered with.
2. Enter your password.

### 3. Choose whether to stay signed in
1. Tick **Remember me** if you are on a private device and would like Process PA to keep you signed in between visits. Leave it unticked on shared or public computers.

### 4. Sign in
1. Select **Log in**. On success you will land on your home page (dashboard). If you belong to more than one organisation, you can switch between them from here.

## If your details don't match

- **Incorrect password** — Process PA shows a warning and lets you try again. If you can't recall your password, use the reset link (see below).
- **Unknown email** — a warning is shown without confirming whether an account exists for that address. This protects members' privacy.

## Moving between login, register and reset

The login page links to the pages you need next:

- **Register as a new user** — creates a fresh account (see [Create an account](/help/onboarding/create%20an%20account)).
- **Forgotten password** — sends a reset link to your email (see [Reset a forgotten password](/help/onboarding/reset%20your%20password)).

You can move freely between these pages without losing your place.

## Tips

- Bookmark [https://app.processpa.com/](https://app.processpa.com/) for quick access.
- Emails sent from Process PA include a link that takes you straight to the relevant meeting after you log in.
