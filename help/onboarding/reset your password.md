## Overview

If you can't remember your password, you can reset it yourself by email. Process PA sends a secure link that lets you choose a new password. For everyone's security, the system never reveals whether an email address belongs to an account.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### 1. Open the reset page
1. From the login page at [https://app.processpa.com/](https://app.processpa.com/), select the **Forgotten password** link.

### 2. Enter your email address
1. Type the email address you use for Process PA.
2. Submit the form.

Whether or not that address has an account, you'll see the same confirmation message. This is deliberate — it stops anyone from using the reset form to discover who has an account.

### 3. Open the reset link
1. Check your inbox for an email from Process PA containing a reset link.
2. If it hasn't arrived within a few minutes, check your junk or spam folder and make sure you [trust the sender](/help/trust-email-sender).
3. Select the link in the email.

### 4. Choose a new password
1. Enter a new password.
2. Confirm it, then save.
3. You can now log in with your new password.

## Tips

- Reset links are time-limited. If yours has expired, simply request another.
- If you never receive an email, the address may differ from the one on your account — try any alternate addresses, or contact [support@processpa.com](mailto:support@processpa.com).
