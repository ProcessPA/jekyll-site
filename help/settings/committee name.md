## Overview

You can rename your committee at any time from its general settings. The new name appears across the app and on your agendas and minutes.

## Step-by-Step Guide

1. Open **Settings** from the menu bar.
2. In general settings, find the committee name field.
3. Enter the new name.
4. Save your change.
