## Overview

Agenda templates give every meeting a consistent structure. If you've built a template that works well, you can copy it to reuse that structure — for example, as the starting point for another meeting type.

## Step-by-Step Guide

1. Open **Settings** from the menu bar.
2. Go to the agenda template area.
3. Choose to copy an existing template.
4. Adjust the copy as needed — rearrange items, add standard motions or actions — and save.

## Good to know

- Templates can include standard motions and actions that are generated for every meeting made from them.
- You can maintain separate templates for General, Annual and Special meetings.
