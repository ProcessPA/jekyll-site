## Overview

You might belong to more than one organisation in Process PA — for example, if you serve on committees for different associations. You can move between them easily, and you'll only ever see the organisations you belong to.

## Step-by-Step Guide

1. Log in at [https://app.processpa.com/](https://app.processpa.com/).
2. Use the organisation switcher to choose which organisation you want to work in.
3. Process PA loads that organisation's committee, dashboard and records.

## How access works

- When you register or are added with an email address, every organisation that address belongs to becomes available to you.
- You see **all** the organisations you belong to — and **only** those. One organisation's members, meetings and documents are never visible to another (tenant isolation).
- A distribution sent "to all" reaches only the members of that committee — there's no crossover between organisations.
