## Overview

The Actions page lists every action item across your committee so nothing slips between meetings. You can review outstanding tasks and create a new action from here.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### 1. Open the Actions page
1. From the menu bar, select **Actions**.
2. Your action items are listed.

### 2. Create a new action
1. Choose to add a new action.
2. Enter the detail — what needs doing and who owns it — and save.

### 3. Carry actions forward
1. Outstanding items from previous meetings automatically appear as business arising on the next agenda, so they stay visible until they're done. See [Confirm minutes and business arising](/help/meetings/confirm%20previous%20minutes).

## Good to know

- Action items can also be added directly against an agenda item while preparing or running a meeting. See [Add action items and motions](/help/meetings/add%20action%20items%20and%20motions).
