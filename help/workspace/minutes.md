## Overview

The Minutes page is your committee's history of completed meetings. Every meeting you have closed appears here, and selecting one shows its full minutes.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### 1. Open the Minutes page
1. From the menu bar, select **Minutes**.
2. You'll see a list of your completed meetings.

### 2. Open a meeting's minutes
1. Select the meeting you're interested in.
2. Its minutes are displayed, including attendance, motions, action items and notes exactly as recorded.

### 3. Preview or download
1. From a completed meeting you can preview the minutes on screen and download the matching PDF. See [Preview and download the PDF](/help/meetings/preview%20and%20download%20pdf).

## Good to know

- Completed meetings can't be deleted by users — this protects the continuity of your records. If you need one removed, contact [support@processpa.com](mailto:support@processpa.com).
- Minutes from completed meetings can still be edited and redistributed, though new action items and motions can't be added after finalization.
