## Overview

The Motions page gathers every motion raised across your committee's meetings in one place, so you can find a decision without hunting through individual minutes.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### 1. Open the Motions page
1. From the menu bar, select **Motions**.
2. A list of motions is displayed.

### 2. Open a motion
1. Select a motion to see its full detail — the wording, who moved and seconded it, and the outcome.

## Good to know

- Motions are created against agenda items while you prepare or run a meeting. See [Add action items and motions](/help/meetings/add%20action%20items%20and%20motions).
- Standard motions can be built into your agenda template so they're generated for every meeting.
